# Recall for Mac

Spaced-repetition flashcards: study decks (including Anki decks) with a modern scheduler that brings each
card back right before you'd forget it.

## Download

**[Download Recall.dmg](https://github.com/Qasim1507/recall-releases/releases/latest/download/Recall.dmg)**

Needs a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or newer) and macOS 12 or later.

1. Open `Recall.dmg` and drag **Recall** onto **Applications**.
2. Open Recall from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the app:
   open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the message about Recall, and click
   **Open Anyway**.

## Updates

Recall tells you when a new version is out and installs it for you when you click **Update Now**. Your
decks and progress are kept, and a backup is made before every update.
